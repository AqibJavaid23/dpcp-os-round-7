Clickable prototype. Fake data only. Branched from `round-2`. Round 1 and round 2 keep their own URLs.

## What landed

- **Knowledge** at `/knowledge/`. Search, SOPs, roles, who does what, systems, and coverage. Sample SOPs, labeled Sample.
- **My Day** on the practice desk, after you tap your name. Front desk, hygiene, assistant, office manager, and doctor each get Preview, To-do, and Completed.
- **Tickets** at `/tickets/`. One request object, a queue per department, sample SLAs, escalation, rating, and analytics. The crown demo now ends with Maria rating the request, then the chair visit still plays.
- **Performance** at `/performance/`. Portfolio, a practice sheet, alerts, and an anonymized HDG benchmark on client rows only.
- **Workforce** at `/workforce/`. My work, the team, the daily report, execution against a goal, and the practice-staff version.
- **Training** at `/training/`. Paths, a short player, a quiz, new hires, and the manager list.
- **Clients** at `/clients/`. Onboarding, sample tiers, invoices, and an upgrade request. Pricing is labeled sample.
- **AI** at `/activity/`. Activity and an approvals inbox. The floating ask mark cites a sample SOP when it can.
- **Admin** at `/admin/`. People, a permissions matrix, the audit log, data controls, devices, integrations, and kill switches.
- **Mobile** at `/mobile/`. Brief, approvals, practices, tickets, and the team.
- **Practice owner** at `/owner/`. Value on top. The Balance Assessment below, with the sequence rule, a plan, Get DPCP help, and a monthly PDF that lands in Review first.
- **Design** at `/design/`, with `docs/design-guidelines.md` and `design/tokens.css`.
