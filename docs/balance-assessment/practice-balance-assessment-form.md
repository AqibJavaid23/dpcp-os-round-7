| THE SEQUENCE RULE / When an imbalance is found in any part, that part becomes the sole intervention priority. Do not work on lower parts until the higher imbalance has a resolution plan in place. A practice cannot convert its way out of a patient flow problem. It cannot collect its way out of a conversion problem. Find the highest point of imbalance. Start there. |
